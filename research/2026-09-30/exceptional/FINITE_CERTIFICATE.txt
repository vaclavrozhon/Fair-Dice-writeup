An explicit finite permutation-fair collection with 21 dice

The distinguished die has 400 faces. Each of the other twenty dice has
the same face count, an integer of bit length 241481. The full word is
specified compactly in finite_n21.json (approximately 254 KB); expanding
it is neither necessary nor feasible.

Inputs and exact recipe:
1. rational_designs/local_model_m20.json gives all rational nodes and
   correction coefficients. finite_n21.json records its SHA-256 digest.
2. The base_word object starts from 2^25 copies of letter0. At step r,
   place the listed runs of new letter r around the listed number of
   copies of the previous word. Its209 monomial moment identities certify
   every insertion step. The resulting20-letter word is permutation-fair
   and has F=2^25(20!)^2 copies of each letter.
3. verify_global_model.construct_cells gives the exact rational cells
   and the400 distinguished atoms, in their numerical order.
4. For each cell with masses alpha_j, substitute the base word and
   repeat each letter j exactly D*alpha_j times. D is the positive common
   denominator stored as mass_denominator_hex in the certificate.
5. At each atom insert the distinguished letter20 once. Give consecutive
   positions in the resulting word the labels1,2,3,... . These are all
   distinct and define the actual finite dice.

All D*alpha_j are positive integers. For each old letter their sum over
cells equals D, so each old die has exactly FD faces. The400 correction
identities, the centered kernel identities, positive densities, and their
normalization are checked over the rational numbers. Fairness of the
entire collection follows from the insertion lemma and the full-order
disjoint-density response lemma. The21! full orders are NOT enumerated.

Reproduce from the repository root:

python3 research/2026-09-30/exceptional/finite_certificate.py verify research/2026-09-30/rational_designs/local_model_m20.json research/2026-09-30/exceptional/finite_n21.json

The verifier does not use floating-point arithmetic or an optimization
solver. The stored finite grammar can be rebuilt with the same command
after replacing 'verify' by 'build'. Build/verification metadata are in
finite_n21_build_summary.json and finite_n21_verify_summary.json.

Mathematical proof:
  ../size_bounds/polynomial_exceptional_die.tex
  ../size_bounds/centered_bump_simplification.tex
  ../size_bounds/factorial_squared_construction.tex

The general theorem supplies one O(n^2)-face die for every n. This one
finite21-dice example is a reproducible check of that construction, not
by itself an asymptotic refutation of an exponential conjecture.

Independent full review of positive_saturation/eventual_primorial_saturation.tex

Verdict: no mathematical gap found.

1. The reduced associative algebra maps to the commutative squarefree
   algebra. Thus an invariant multilinear Lie polynomial of degree k>1
   has every associative coefficient equal to b, while abelianization
   gives k! b=0 on each support. This proves the required vanishing.
2. In the symmetrization step all degree-one logarithms are the same
   scalar multiple of S. Their mutual brackets vanish exactly. Every
   bracket involving an existing degree-k error has degree >=k+1.
   Averaging therefore kills the leading error without introducing any
   lower one. Identity-first concatenation preserves the literal prefix.
3. Repeating degrees 2 through n gives the exact stated count
   B(n!)^(n-1), with positivity maintained throughout.
4. Completion of u and v to T_A and T_B gives common right multiple
   T_(A+B). The fraction multiplication order
   (a b^-1)(c d^-1)=a x(d y)^-1 when b x=c y is correct.
5. Completing denominator b yields g T_L positive. No fair element is
   moved through a general group element, so noncentrality is harmless.
6. The signed-signature input applies precisely to primorial multiples.
   Clearing T_P supplies feasible counts L and L+P. Their normalized
   sizes are consecutive positive integers, and the stated elementary
   Frobenius argument gives all sufficiently large primorial multiples.

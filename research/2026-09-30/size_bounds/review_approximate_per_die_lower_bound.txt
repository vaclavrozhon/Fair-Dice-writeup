Result: PASS, with a minor explicit-model wording request.

Checked every displayed bound. The coefficient for one designated
failure is (k+1)/m, the bad-prefix comparison includes zero entries,
and the nonnormalized moment induction gives the stated error.
The choice d=floor(H/(16 log 3)) makes both Laguerre errors vanish
uniformly as H grows. The conversion from the posterior endpoint atom
to the original row weight is correct. For bounded H, the mass bound
eta(G)>=1/4 at m>=36 and the exponential moment at z=1/2 imply a good
row with lambda<=16; this supplies the necessary uniform linear bound.
Complementing columns and reversing rows preserves the hypotheses.

The dice corollary should explicitly retain the paper's convention of
no ties between different dice. Without that convention, strict-order
bounds alone do not make a comparison-pattern event exactly the stated
union of strict orders. The underlying monotone-tensor theorem has no
such issue. This wording request was sent to the author.

This is an independent mathematical review, not formal verification.

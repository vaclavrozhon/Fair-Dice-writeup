Full independent mathematical review of
independent_directions/optimized_two_adic_filter.tex

Verdict: no gap found. This proves the stated lower bound
K >= 2^(2 floor(r/2)) for rational equal-weight interval designs.

1. The reciprocal Gregory generating series has reduction 1+t modulo 2,
   so every coefficient of G(-2t) is odd and v_2(g_j)=-j exactly.
2. In the dilation composition, each factor of degree below N contributes
   an even binomial coefficient. For j=N ell the only contribution of
   valuation -ell is g_ell z^(N ell). Every term with more factors has
   at most ell-1 factors of top degree N and hence strictly larger valuation.
3. After evaluating at z=-2, each individual expanded degree-j contribution
   has valuation at least j-floor(j/N), which tends to infinity. There are
   only finitely many contributions at each j, so the entire double series
   is unconditionally convergent and regrouping by outer power is valid.
4. For even s and N a positive power of 2, floor((s+1)/N)=floor(s/N).
   This supplies exactly the divisibility needed to correct the integral
   by 2^s times a 2-adically integer-valued binomial polynomial.
5. The case N>s legitimately uses j0=0, a constant correction; its integral
   is one and it preserves all parity congruences.
6. Exactness applies to the rational corrected polynomial, including its
   constant term. The sum of its values is zero, so the positive count of
   maximal-denominator-depth nodes is divisible by 2^s, yielding K>=2^s.
7. The all-integral-node alternative follows directly from the degree-s
   Gregory coefficient; odd r is handled by restricting to the previous
   even degree. No sign, location, or distinctness restriction on the nodes
   is used.

The companion check_optimized_filter.py independently builds all integrals
over Fraction and tests the resulting congruences on rational 2-adic integers.
This review does not claim historical novelty or formal machine verification.

Additional review: odd-degree corollary, latest version.
No gap found. The function j-floor(j/N) is strictly above its value at
a multiple j=r+1 for every later j, giving the unique minimal tail
valuation. When N does not divide r+1 the original null correction
extends unchanged. In the resonant case the congruence with U=2^(r-1)
forces v_2(K)=(r+1)/N-1, so the next possible positive K above 2^(r-1)
has the claimed additive gap. Integral nodes are covered separately.

Additional review: sign_changes_monotone_tensor.tex, sparse-plateau lemma.
No gap found. Consecutive nonzero sign-change intervals have disjoint
edge sets. Thus each function can fail at most b cut adjacencies, and
each cut fails at most F function adjacencies. A nonempty set of at most
b cuts has at least b neighbors; a set of more than b cuts has all d
neighbors. This includes b=0. Hall's theorem supplies the required
matching. At K=m, both nonzero differences strictly alternate on the
whole support, so their product has constant nonzero sign, contradicting
its zero expectation. The graph and rational-rule conclusions follow.

Diagnostic of a proposed asymptotic lower-bound route (30 September 2026)

The proposed universal lemma was: every finite positive fair word is a
regular point of its face-weight endpoint map in the reduced Lie group.
It would imply length >= dim(Lie_red(n)) ~ e(n-1)!, and hence settle the
exponential-versus-factorial size question. The lemma is FALSE.

For the already published Paul Meyer five-die fair word (60 faces each),
the exact face-weight Jacobian has rank 78, whereas dim Lie_red(5)=89.
This is a diagnostic counterexample to the universal asymptotic argument,
not a search for an improved small-die construction.

Moreover, its right-trivialized tangent space S is exactly invariant under
ad_H, H=X_1+...+X_5. For a word with signature exp(60H), the tangent space
of its r-fold concatenation is sum_{j=0}^{r-1} exp(60j ad_H)S. Therefore
EVERY positive integer power still has rank 78; bounded/unbounded ordinary
repetition does not repair the proposed argument.

Reproduction:
  python3 certify_endpoint_singularity.py
  python3 certify_power_singularity.py
Run in this directory. Both scripts regenerate the entire Jacobian from
the literal published word. Rank >=78 is certified by an invertible
78x78 minor modulo a prime. Rank <=78 and ad_H-invariance are certified
by exact integer factorizations with a common denominator, not numerical
rank thresholds. The JSON files record pivot rows, pivot faces, and the
common denominator. No conclusion about the true asymptotic optimum follows.

30 September 2026, 18:20 UTC: the stronger all-gap conjecture is false too.
Allow a new infinitesimal face of every letter at every gap of the same
published fair word. The 325-by-1505 integer endpoint Jacobian has rank
exactly 86, below the ambient reduced Lie dimension 89. The script
certify_all_gap_singularity.py proves the upper bound by an exact integer
factorization through an invertible 86-by-86 minor; the minor is also
invertible modulo 1000000007. The separate script
certify_all_gap_power_singularity.py right-trivializes the tangent and
proves, again by exact integer factorization, that it is invariant under
ad_H. Therefore all positive powers have the same all-gap rank 86.

This is an asymptotic obstruction to the proposed universal lemma, not
just a fixed-dimensional anomaly. Extend this fair word to arbitrarily
many letters by the standard one-letter insertion construction. Deleting
all new letters leaves a positive integer power of the original word.
Under the Lie-group projection deleting those letters, every all-gap
insertion tangent of the extended word projects either to zero (for a
new letter) or into the all-gap tangent span of that old power. A full
ambient tangent span would project surjectively to the old 89-dimensional
Lie algebra, whereas its image has dimension at most 86. Thus there are
positive fair words with singular all-gap endpoint maps for arbitrarily
large alphabet size. No lower bound on the relative corank is claimed.

This diagnostic rules out full regularity as a route to the factorial
size lower bound. It does not rule out a weaker universal lower bound on
the tangent rank by a positive fraction of the ambient dimension.

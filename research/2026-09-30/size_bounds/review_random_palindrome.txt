Independent review: random palindrome approximate-fairness bounds
Reviewer: size_bounds agent, 30 September 2026

Files reviewed in full:
  approximate_random/random_palindrome_bound.tex
  approximate_random/refined_random_palindrome_bound.tex

Verdict: no mathematical gap found in either proof. The refined result gives
O(n^(13/10) (log n)^(1/5) epsilon^(-2/5)) faces per die, uniformly over all
full permutation probabilities in relative error.

Specific checks for the refined proof:
1. A prescribed k-letter subsequence occurs at most twice in a palindrome
   of a permutation. Thus |delta| <= k! 2^(1-k), k>=3; pairs vanish exactly.
2. Given a block's selected size and residue class of starts modulo that
   size, all retained segment variables involve disjoint priority sets.
   Across blocks they are independent as well. This supplies a single
   independent family to which the multilinear chaos lemma applies.
3. Every term is retained with probability product(q_k/k). Multiplying its
   coefficient by product(k/q_k), symmetrizing, and applying Jensen to the
   square root leaves exactly product(k*b_k^2/q_k) in the coefficient
   square sum. Duplicate monomials are absent under the segment indexing.
4. The multinomial coefficient formula is correct, including the factor
   (M/m)^d. The map between selected block sets and weak gap compositions
   is a bijection. Segment starts and label counts in gaps correspond.
5. The lattice collision estimate correctly handles d=0 and zero gaps.
   Poisson conditioning gives the stated product upper bound; selecting
   one gap at least M/(ell+1) and dropping the other sum constraints is
   valid. The final constant 108 covers 6(ell+1)^(3/2) times 6^ell.
6. On short support M>=d+2ell and d>=n/2, so the lattice lemma applies.
   The length generating function simplifies to sum(k*z^k), z=n^2/(2m^2),
   and is at most 8z^3 for z<=1/2. This yields n^(11/4)/m^(5/2).
7. On long support, inserting theta^(s-n/2) is an upper bound; its square
   root gives theta^(-n/4). With t=m/n, theta=t^(1/3), the power-series
   argument is 1/(2t^(5/3)) and A_theta^2<=4pn/t^4. Cauchy--Schwarz gives
   sum A^ell/sqrt(ell!) <= sqrt(2)*exp(A^2).
8. Under the theorem hypothesis, a<=epsilon/6 and 4p/t^4<=1/1024<=log(t)/24.
   For n>=60, sqrt(2)*t^(-n/24)<=epsilon/128; together with the short part
   this is below epsilon/3. The old blockwise estimate covers n<60 with
   substantial slack. Markov and the n! union bound then give probability
   at least 1/2 exactly as claimed.

No assertion of optimality or novelty follows from this review. The proof
is existential with a positive success probability, not an efficient
certificate for simultaneously checking n! output probabilities.

Additional complete review: random_palindrome_lower_bound.tex
Verdict: no gap found. Specific checks:
- The isolated six-priority event (6,1,3,2,4,5) changes exactly one valley,
  and its reverse event gives variance at least w_i(u)^2/160.
- Swaps in disjoint six-position windows affect disjoint priority sets.
  Their effects are additive even when all swaps are selected together.
- The central binomial-weight sum is of order m/n^(3/2); the unimodal
  Riemann-sum error is negligible uniformly for m>=n.
- Fourth moments, interpolation to the first absolute moment, and the
  independent-window Chebyshev estimate give the n^(13/4)/m^(5/2) range.
- Removing size-three terms from level one leaves a/t; higher levels
  contribute O(a^2). The long-support bound is uniform even for arbitrarily
  large m, and is <=A/t for sufficiently large n.
- With p=4n the binary-family union bound controls the complete nonlinear
  remainder. Constants can be chosen in the stated order.
- The restriction corollary for m=o(n^(13/10)) is valid: marginal relative
  errors are averages of full-order relative errors.
- The covariance values and finite-difference variance decomposition agree
  exactly, including both off-diagonal factors of two.

Additional complete review: gaussian_limit_log_factor.tex
Verdict: no gap found. The iterated-limit qualification is essential and
correctly stated. I checked the finite-dimensional Lindeberg normalization,
L^2 disappearance of all nontriple terms, uniform integrability for fixed n,
the three-variable ANOVA variance 1/20, orthogonality of all N^2 Gaussian
assignment entries, the exact orthogonal projection onto their span, Gaussian
independence of the residual, conditional Jensen, and greedy assignment.
The result establishes the logarithm in the large-m Gaussian limit only;
it does not establish a uniform Gaussian approximation near the threshold.

Additional complete review: finite_log_lower_bound.tex
Verdict: no gap found. This closes the preceding uniformity limitation
inside the stated random-palindrome family. Specific checks:
- The soft maximum third directional derivative is bounded by
  8 beta^2 ||v||_infinity^3. Matching means and covariances therefore
  justifies the blockwise Lindeberg comparison in dimension L!.
- The exact covariance row sums give sum_i Var X_i(pi) <= C L.
  This uses the summed binomial coefficient squares, rather than only
  the coarser maximum coefficients M_i (which would lose a factor L).
- The normalized comparison error is at most
  C L^3 (log L)^2 n^(1/4)/sqrt(m); with L=floor(n^(1/10)) this is
  o(1) uniformly throughout the theorem's m regime.
- The Gaussian assignment component is orthogonal to its residual, so
  conditional Jensen and greedy assignment give the required mean range.
- Windows of length 3L+4 use disjoint priority sets. Their ranges add
  exactly over the product family, and the Bernstein second-moment
  bound gives the claimed failure probability O(n^(-9/10)).
- The full nonlinear remainder is uniform over the entire product
  family with p=4n log n. Its relative size is negligible once the
  absolute lower threshold constant on m is sufficiently large.
- Restriction to a suitable smaller letter set proves the stated
  subthreshold corollary for fixed epsilon.
This is mathematical peer review, not a machine-checked formal proof.

Additional complete review: minwise_discrepancy_barrier.tex
Verdict: no gap found. The averaged pair marginals supply the distinguished
row even without pairwise balance. The projected Gram matrix has positive
trace at least (m-1)(1/6-epsilon), is PSD of rank at most q, and has the
claimed Frobenius bound. Rearranging the rank inequality yields the stated
1/(32 sqrt(q)) constant when m>=64q. Restricting to unused labels preserves
support size <=q, and the three minimum probabilities sum to one, giving
the disjoint positive-deviation corollary. The final scope statement
correctly distinguishes a short-interval discrepancy barrier from a lower
bound on all deterministic palindrome constructions.

Additional complete review: deterministic_swap_discrepancy.tex
Verdict: no gap found. Extending delta by zero preserves both endpoint
symmetry and the center-sum identity. I rederived s_c=-r_c/2, the formula
for V, and the average over d in (3). The final coboundary identity is
exact: its U coefficients are -1/2 on cb,bc,cd,dc and +1 on bd,db,
and its r coefficients are +1/2 at c and -1/4 at b,d. The exceptional
collision estimates 72/n, 24/n, and 14/n are safe. Subtracting the additive
rank-two matrix gives exactly the stated diagonal d_ij; trace/Frobenius
control needs symmetry, not positivity, and rank <=q+2 is enough.
The chosen numerical n/q threshold is more than sufficient. Swapping
positions 3 and 4 of a six-label window affects exactly its four internal
triples, so independent windows add their ranges without boundary terms.
The claimed result remains explicitly a linear path-score discrepancy
statement, not a lower bound for full deterministic dice error.

Additional complete review: weighted_collision_discrepancy.tex and the
revised deterministic_binomial_projection.tex (weighted, no truncation).
Verdict: no gap found. The centered Gram collision expansion has
nonnegative cross terms, and averaging the diagonal terms over all
pivots gives exactly (m-1)(m^2+1)/(30m) >=m^2/60 for m>=2. Projection
kills every term of the additive coboundary matrix. The resulting
Frobenius comparison and numerical coefficients are correct, including
restriction with repeated block permutations.
The beta integral for squared central binomial weights, bounded-variation
grid errors, and derivative identity for adjacent starts all check.
At m>=n the discretization errors are smaller than the relevant main
terms. At m<=c0*n^(3/2), both the weighted collision threshold and the
absorption of the O(n^(-1/2)) normalized weight-shift error hold after
choosing a sufficiently small absolute c0. There is space for floor(n/40)
disjoint six-position windows in the central third; all their affected
triple starts stay central. Filling windows with unused labels preserves
at least n/2 labels at every stage. Their binary ranges add exactly,
and h is bounded below by a constant times n^3/m^3 when m>=n. The final
power n^(13/4)/m^(5/2) is correct. The scope is properly restricted to
the deterministic linear projection, with no assertion about cancellation
by the complete nonlinear ordering error.

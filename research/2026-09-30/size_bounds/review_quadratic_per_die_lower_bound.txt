30 September 2026: quadratic per-die lower bound

The complete five-page proof in quadratic_per_die_lower_bound.tex was
independently read by /root, /root/rational_designs, and
/root/independent_directions. All three reviews PASS, with no gap found.

Each reviewer checked the all-orders off-diagonal Gaussian identity
(fixed total matrix index d+1), uniform factorial normalization, entire
polarization series summation, improved subset bias/variance, weighted
bad-event normalization, order of choosing the absolute small kappa,
both concentration regimes, large-x exponential margin, cutoff Stieltjes
integrals including atoms, and the final U_(d/16) test.

All three independently noted the same harmless domain mismatch in the
derivative lemma: its original statement had z>=1/d while intermediate
points can be as small as 1/(2d). This is corrected in statement and
proof; the bound changes only by an absolute sqrt(2) factor. No other
correction was needed.

Conclusion: every equiprobable die in a tie-free permutation-fair family
has Omega(n^2) faces. Together with polynomial_exceptional_die.tex this
establishes g(n)=Theta(n^2), where all other dice may be arbitrarily large.
It does not settle minimum total size or polynomial equalization.

This is mathematical peer review, not machine formal verification or an
assertion of literature novelty. main.tex was not modified.

The fully reviewed original dependency version is preserved as

replaces its one-step classical localized-Laguerre CDF input by the same
all-orders Gaussian argument with the older coarse balance. That

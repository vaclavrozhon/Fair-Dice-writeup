THE THREE QUESTIONS IN THE MANUSCRIPT

1. Minimum total length A_n: exp(O(n)) versus exp(Theta(n log n)).
   OPEN. No general upper bound exp(O(n)) or lower bound exp(Omega(n log n))
   has been proved during this research. The equal-face construction
   C(n!)^2 improves the explicit insertion bound but does not close the gap.

2. Polynomial equalization E_n <= A_n^C for an absolute C.
   OPEN. Combining the factorial-squared upper bound and the exponential
   lower bound gives E_n <= A_n^{O(log n)}, equivalently
   A_n^{O(log log A_n)}. Simple lcm equalization and positive block averaging
   have substantial barriers, but these are not impossibility proofs.

3. Must every individual die have exponentially many faces?
   NEGATIVE, with a proved general construction: one die can have O(n^2)
   faces for every n; the remaining n-1 dice may have the same, much larger,
   number of faces. In fact EVERY integer K>=C*n^2 can be prescribed as
   that die's exact face count. The total-size questions remain unaffected.
   MAJOR UPGRADE: proved universal lower bound c*n^2 faces per die.
   Together with the construction, this determines g(n)=Theta(n^2).
   The replacement individual-die optimization is asymptotically solved.
   The full proof has independent reviews by all three research agents;
   root wrote the combined proof and reviewed its companion inputs.

   Full proof: size_bounds/polynomial_exceptional_die.tex.
   Every-size extension: rational_designs/asymptotic/every_large_exceptional_size.tex.
   Simplified correction lemma: size_bounds/centered_bump_simplification.tex.
   Current lower bound: size_bounds/quadratic_per_die_lower_bound.tex.
   Earlier localized bootstrap: size_bounds/near_quadratic_per_die_bootstrap.tex.
   Foundational comparison and n^(3/2) bound: power_per_die_lower_bound.tex.
   Earlier lower bound: size_bounds/logarithmic_per_die_lower_bound.tex.
   Structural limit: independent_directions/endpoint_poisson_rigidity.tex.

   Mechanism: finite balance of all mean deficits; a symmetric
   forced-success tilt; stable multiaffine polarization using its actual
   variance; off-diagonal Gaussian/Laguerre derivative bounds; weighted
   subset bias and variance. Scaled Laguerre tests retain an exponential
   margin. The structured bilinear error is C*B1*B2*d/n. A recurrence
   factorization of the endpoint taper gives B1*B2=O(d), permitting
   degree d=c*sqrt(n), endpoint tilted mass O(1/d), and face weight
   O(1/(n*d)). An elementary all-orders Gaussian estimate improves the endpoint
   CDF cutoff from m^-1/2 to m^-3/4; no external Laguerre asymptotic
   theorem is needed in the current proof. A second input is relative balance
   max_i m*u_i<=C(s+m^(-1/3)). Applying the Gaussian matrix estimate
   directly to associated Laguerre polynomials yields uniform bounds for
   ALL derivatives of the special taper. This sums the whole polarization
   error at degree d=kappa*m, giving endpoint x=O(1/m) and atom O(1/m^2).
   The improved balance controls rare bad subset averages. All constants
   in this final bound are absolute. It uses the full comparison tensor,
   so the generic approximate-moment barrier does not apply.

  rational_designs/asymptotic/uniform_endpoint_profile.tex.

RECENT STRUCTURAL RESULTS AND METHOD BARRIERS

* Scalar transfer holds locally at regular scalar boundary points, and
  at endpoint boundary points with strict normal derivative signs. Deep
  few-level strata also have local rigidity. No global transfer theorem
  follows: intermediate singular strata and disconnected components
  have not been excluded.
  independent_directions/scalar_transfer_boundary.tex;
  rational_designs/asymptotic/scalar_transfer_endpoint_boundary.tex.

* Positivity and normalized exponential moments accurate to delta alone
  permit an endpoint atom of order 1/log(1/delta), even with every moment
  controlled. An explicit positive Laguerre construction proves this.
  The power lower bound now supplies exactly such additional structure.
  rational_designs/asymptotic/logarithmic_moment_barrier.tex.

* The one-correction-per-row model underlying the polynomial exceptional
  die requires Omega(n^2) rows, provided its scalar baseline nodes are
  distinct. With at most d corrections per row, a conflict-graph argument
  gives Omega(min(n^2,n^(4/3)/d^(4/3))). These are construction-method
  barriers only; they do not close the general g(n) gap.
  sparse_row_correction_barrier.tex.

* Walsh/code symmetrization gives exactly fair rational 0/2 densities
  on exp(O(n)) equal cells. Every activity subset occurs, including the
  full set. Finite realization on smaller alphabets is therefore circular;
  no exp(O(n)) finite-dice construction follows.
  size_bounds/walsh_positive_splitting.tex.

  all-orders Laguerre argument; the full transfer statement remains open.
  The permanent-polynomial obstruction is a

  EVERY FIXED k is now proved, with exponent depending on k; see below. Arithmetic
  limits the number of n^C-size dice to O_C(log n), by applying the
  exponential maximum-die bound to their own subfamily.

* Fair rational step densities with only half the dice active in each
  cell, exponentially many cells, and exponentially bounded denominators.
  Such a construction would give a divide-and-conquer exp(O(n)) upper
  bound. Existence at that complexity is entirely unproved.

* Odd-degree rational quadrature: the candidate scale is
  R_{2k+1} = 2^{2k}+O(2^k), but there is no upper-bound proof. A possible
  route is a nonsingular 2-adic solution with K=2^{2k}+2^k and nodes in
  (1/2)Z_2; the corresponding integer targets are K*2^j/(j+1).
  The published uniform local bound does not establish this special case.

* Endpoint regularity as a factorial lower-bound argument has FAILED in
  its universal formulation. Exact tangent-rank certificates show that a
  positive fair word, and all its powers, can have a singular endpoint map,
  even when insertion in every gap is allowed. Projection preserves the
  defect after adding arbitrary further dice, so the obstruction extends
  to arbitrarily large n. This rejects a proposed proof, not a size bound.
  size_bounds/all_gap_singularity_inheritance.tex.

Two specified dice can simultaneously have polynomially many faces in
a completely finite permutation-fair n-die family. The current explicit

potentially enormous face count. All three full independent reviews PASS.

Main theorem: size_bounds/two_polynomial_exceptional_dice.tex.

Inputs: rational_designs/asymptotic/two_discrete_step_compatibility.tex;
independent_directions/two_exceptional_positive_completion.tex.

Mechanism: a quantitatively conditioned Legendre/implicit-function
construction gives separated B nodes with rational A prefix ranks.
Rational affine densities in tiny boxes around B preserve ordinary and
rank-weighted moments. A positive rational polynomial density lives in
the complementary gaps, obtained by a stable finite-dimensional Riesz
completion. Thus both distributions have rational moments and masses on
disjoint pure clusters. Cellwise real quadrature yields polynomial face
counts with enough distinct nodes per cluster. Rational approximation
and disjoint moment bumps reproduce every cluster functional exactly;
surviving mixed terms equal the corresponding mixed boundary derivatives.
Finally the other dice are made finite by rational interval fair blocks.
Arbitrarily small box widths affect only those other dice's denominators.

This still does not change the total-size upper bound, does not make all
dice polynomial, and does not solve polynomial equalization. The sharp
simultaneous two-die minimum remains between c*n^2 and C*n^162.

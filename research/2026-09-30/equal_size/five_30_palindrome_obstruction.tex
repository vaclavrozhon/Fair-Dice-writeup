\begin{proposition}
No concatenation of fifteen blocks of the form
$\rho\,\operatorname{rev}(\rho)$, where each $\rho$ is a permutation
of five labels, is permutation-fair.
\end{proposition}

This is a restricted construction obstruction. It does not rule out
arbitrary five30-sided permutation-fair dice. Its literature priority
has not been established: earlier work already mentions exclusions of
structured subclasses.

\begin{proof}[Proof by an exact integer certificate]
Work in the associative algebra in which words with a repeated letter
vanish. Write $S=X_0+\cdots+X_4$ and let $P_\rho$ be the subsequence
signature of the palindrome block. Every block is two-wise fair, so
\[
 P_\rho=\exp(2S)+D_\rho,\qquad \deg D_\rho\ge3.
\]
Products of two defects have degree at least six. Thus, for blocks at
positions $i=0,\ldots,14$, the degree-five defect of their product is
exactly the sum of
\[
 \exp(2iS)D_\rho\exp(2(14-i)S).
\]

For a distinct-letter pattern $q$, let $N_\rho(q)$ be its subsequence
count in the block and put
\[
 d_3(q)=3N_\rho(q)-4,\quad
 d_4(q)=3N_\rho(q)-2,\quad
 d_5(q)=15N_\rho(q)-4.
\]
For each length-five pattern $p=p_1\cdots p_5$ and $j=14-i$, the
integer column recording fifteen times this transported defect is
\begin{align*}
 c_{i,\rho}(p)={}&d_5(p)+10i\,d_4(p_2\cdots p_5)
   +10j\,d_4(p_1\cdots p_4)\\
 &+10i^2d_3(p_3p_4p_5)+20ij\,d_3(p_2p_3p_4)
   +10j^2d_3(p_1p_2p_3).
\end{align*}
Fairness therefore requires $\sum_i c_{i,\rho_i}=0$.
Global relabeling allows us to fix $\rho_0=(0,1,2,3,4)$.

The accompanying file \path{five_30_farkas.json} supplies an integer
vector $\lambda$ indexed by the120 patterns, with all entries between
$-20$ and $20$. Direct integer evaluation gives
\[
 \lambda\cdot c_{0,\mathrm{id}}
   +\sum_{i=1}^{14}\min_{\rho\in S_5}
                      \lambda\cdot c_{i,\rho}=40080>0.
\]
Every proposed sequence consequently has a strictly positive scalar
product of its total defect with $\lambda$, and cannot have zero total
defect. The independent program
\path{verify_five_30_obstruction.py} regenerates all1680 remaining
columns and verifies the displayed integer inequality. It needs only
the Python standard library, no numerical solver.
\end{proof}

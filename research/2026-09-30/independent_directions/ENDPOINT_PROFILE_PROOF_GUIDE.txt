Final statement
--------------
natural_scale_endpoint_profile.tex (+PDF): for every fixed L<infinity,
  max_i |m(1-p_i)-s| <= C_L (sqrt(s/m)+1/m),
  s=sum_i(1-p_i), 0<=s<=L.
Together with the trivial bound m*s, the optimal envelope is
  R_m(s)=min(m*s, sqrt(s/m)+1/m).
intermediate_endpoint_sharpness.tex constructs actual finite uniform fair
dice, for every lambda in (0,L], with s comparable to lambda and deviation
at least c_L R_m(lambda). This is scale comparability, not an exact
prescription of s, and no face-count bound is asserted for the examples.

The comparison columns are monotone, their row weights may be arbitrary
positive probabilities, and all mixed moments equal 1/(|S|+1).
No rationality or equal row weights are required. The estimate now includes
s=0 and the inverse-dimension boundary region. It does not claim constants
uniform for a growing upper window L.

For s>0 the relative error is at most
  C_L ((m*s)^(-1/2)+(m*s)^(-1)).
Thus relative synchronization holds whenever m*s tends to infinity and
s remains bounded. Reversing rows and replacing p_i by 1-p_i gives the
same statement at the opposite endpoint. The condition m*s -> infinity
is sharp for a uniform relative conclusion: lambda=1/m gives actual
examples with s comparable to 1/m and relative error bounded away from zero.

Minimal new proof order
-----------------------
1. marked_cancellation_identity.tex
   Exact success/failure marked identity; centered elementary coefficient
   bound; range-sensitive weighted-subset bias/variance; concentration
   under every product-weight tilt; localized Taylor comparison.
   The crucial cancellation removes the scalar approximation error:
   every local marked error is at least quadratic in the column range.
   The last Poisson-specific differential-sum lemma is not needed for the
   log-free branch.

2. ../bernstein_walsh_polarization_tail.tex
   Elementary all-order Bernstein--Walsh/Cauchy derivative bound.
   Centered variance cancels its endpoint factor; success weights control
   the upper half of the polynomial interval. Differential orders above
   B log(m) have arbitrarily small global residual for degree kappa*m.

3. ../rational_designs/asymptotic/finite_jackson_endpoint_kernel.tex
   Positive ordinary polynomial from a symmetric pair of fixed-power
   Fejer kernels under x=(ell/2)(1-cos(theta)). It has width m^(-1/2),
   compact polynomial spatial tails, and all-order localized derivatives.
   Includes centered and success-weighted comparisons, far-angle Cauchy
   estimates through B log(m), and the global off-annulus differential
   tail. The fixed Jackson power is chosen large; it does not grow with m.

4. optimal_interior_endpoint_profile.tex (fixed positive lower cutoff)
   Assembles actual unmarked/marked comparisons, proves local mass
   absorption with polynomial tails, and applies a weighted maximum in
   the common coordinate y=(ell/m)s. The exact identities are
      x_i=y-h_i*/(m-1), h_i=m h_i*/(m-1).
   A center shifted O(m^(-1/2)) to the favorable side captures a fixed
   amount of kernel mass. The marked error is
      O(Delta^2/sqrt(m) + Delta^3)=o(Delta).
   The Jackson power is chosen after the prior convergence exponent, so
   all weighted-cutoff boundary tails are negligible. Global scalar
   Jackson tails are treated separately from the compact envelope.

5. ../rational_designs/asymptotic/variable_jackson_endpoint_kernel.tex
   Uniform center normalization, width sqrt(a/m)+1/m, capped local
   derivatives, and relative off-annulus differential tails. The latter
   use the global sharp CDF, not the total-mass bound alone. Complex
   angular shifts may exceed theta_a: the global trigonometric estimate
   already absorbs their full exp(C*k) cost.

6. natural_scale_endpoint_profile.tex
   First obtains a uniform upper local-mass bound by absorption, then
   shrinks the small-center window for a positive lower kernel mass.
   Maximizes |h_i*|/(sqrt(y/m)+1/m), using sharp balance at m*y=O(1)
   and step 4 at fixed positive y. The marked error at the maximizing
   scale is Delta^2/sqrt(m*a)+Delta^3/a, which contracts because
   Delta<=C*a. This proves the final bound including s=0.

7. intermediate_endpoint_sharpness.tex
   Gaussian node index j=ceil(sqrt(m*lambda)) and uniform Legendre
   zero/Christoffel bounds supply fluctuations sqrt(lambda/m) for
   lambda in [1/m,L]. The mth exact moment controls the Gaussian tail.
   Adjustable Legendre endpoint perturbations cover every smaller
   positive lambda. ../finite_profile_realization.tex transfers each
   robust profile to exactly fair finite uniform dice. These examples
   prove sharpness up to constants and comparability of the deficit scale.

8. ../rational_designs/asymptotic/natural_scale_row_atoms.tex
   A separate corollary gives row weight
     w_q <= C_L (sqrt(s_q)/m^(3/2)+1/m^2).
   It combines endpoint local mass with a positive success-product lower
   bound. Gaussian and Lobatto rules prove matching weighted-tensor
   examples; no matching actual uniform-die face-count claim is made.

Sharpness and remaining scope
-----------------------------
The all-scales lower examples in step 7 are fully finite and equiprobable.
Their face counts may be arbitrarily large. They prescribe a deficit
within fixed factors of lambda, not s=lambda exactly. Earlier root notes
endpoint_profile_fluctuation_barrier.tex, finite_profile_realization.tex
and endpoint_profile_sharpness.tex remain useful simpler special cases.
The constants in the upper and lower estimates depend on the fixed upper
window L; growing-window uniformity is not asserted.

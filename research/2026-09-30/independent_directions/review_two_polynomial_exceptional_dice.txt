Rational smoothing:
- The nonconstant ordinary and rank-weighted slope-response matrix divided
  by epsilon converges to one third of the already conditioned node Jacobian.
- Symmetric box errors are O(epsilon^2), center rounding O(epsilon^3),
  so a fixed full-rank minor gives rational affine slopes O(epsilon).
- Box masses remain exactly 1/K and density ratios are uniformly bounded.

Complementary A completion:
- A polynomial/step combination constant in every gap has, for each
  nonconstant power, coefficient vector annihilated by all B box moment rows.
  The Vandermonde perturbation has full rank, proving the exact step kernel.
- The centered polynomial/step basis is independent at zero width and small
  positive widths. Its Gram and RHS converge, so the Riesz correction and
  its cellwise sup norms converge to the reviewed positive atomic completion.
- The RHS annihilates steps exactly by ordinary and rational-rank moments.
- All actual matrices, endpoints, densities, targets, and masses are rational.
- Triangle degree-m moment identities imply all full order probabilities.

Atomization and counts:
- GP's every-N theorem at degree 2m+2 applies with uniform constants.
- There are at least m+2 distinct support points initially, hence m interior
  pivots. Vandermonde IFT preserves degree-m moments while moving boundary
  nodes inward and separating all repeats. Collision equalities are not
  identically forced: a free node coinciding with a pivot has first-order
  pivot response -1, so their difference derivative is nonzero.
- Counts KT=O(m^6), LT=O(m^162), and every cluster has >=m^2 distinct nodes.
- Cluster moments through degree m stay rational despite real node positions.

Clustered lifting:
- Full-moment cancellation kills any uncut support.
- Surviving supports correspond injectively to specified boundaries and old
  labels. Mixed derivatives have exactly the same boundary-label accounting;
  in a gap with one old variable the second derivative is zero, so no forbidden
  reuse of the same old label occurs.
- Common signed moment functionals per cluster factor all surviving terms.
- Rational baseline cluster moments make the small correction functionals
  rational; fixed distinct-node Vandermonde inverses and fixed support kernels
  keep corrections small enough for positivity.
- Final fair-block finiteization of rational old interval masses preserves
  every complete order and gives one common finite count for all old dice.

Conclusion: two polynomial-size exceptional dice exist in a completely finite
permutation-fair family for every n>=3. No polynomial bound for the other dice
and no optimal quadratic two-die upper bound are claimed.

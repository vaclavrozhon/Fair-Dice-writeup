                                                                               Abstract
                                                   We prove the existence of locally distance increasing maps with con-
                                               trollably small curvatures between Riemannian manifolds, where our main
                                               construction depends on the presence of particular spherical and almost
                                               spherical sections of the unit balls in the lp=4 spaces.
                                                   In the part II [Gr 2022’] we prove similar results for C ∞ -smooth iso-
                                               metric immersions X m → Y N , where our approach allows an improvement
                                               of the present-day bounds on the dimension N of the ambient manifold
                                               Y in certain cases.

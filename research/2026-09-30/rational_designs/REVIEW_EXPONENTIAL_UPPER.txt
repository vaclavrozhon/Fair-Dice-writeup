Independent review of size_bounds/exponential_rational_designs.tex
Reviewer: rational_designs agent, 30 September 2026.

The complete proof was read independently, including its final large-prime
fixed-pivot version and the exact statement of Cui--Xia--Xiang Theorem 5.2
in the downloaded primary paper. No gap was found.

1. Integral polynomial remainder is Lipschitz with an additional p^(b+1)
   on the proposed ball; multiplication by A^-1 loses at most p^b. Thus
   the fixed-Jacobian contraction is valid and preserves nonsingularity.
2. Complete residue power sums have valuation >=a-1, by induction using
   x+t p^a. The Lagrange inverse denominators divide (r-1)!; diagonal j
   loses at most floor(log_p r). Therefore a=2b+2 suffices. The available
   number p^a of initial coordinates exceeds r.
3. Scaling a nonsingular homogeneous zero reduces any rational p-adic
   target vector to a sufficiently small target. Padding by zeros is valid.
4. For p<=4r^2, p^(2b+2)<=4r^6 4^r, using p^(1/(p-1))<=2.
5. For p>4r^2, fixing r coordinates 0,...,r-1 gives a unit Jacobian minor.
   With L=K-r>=4r^2-r free coordinates, character orthogonality plus Weil
   bounds the relative counting error by (r-1)^L p^(r-L/2), strictly below
   (2r)^(2r)2^(-L)<1. L>2r^2 and log_2(2r)<=r justify the last inequality.
6. The degree-(2r+2) real quadrature input has at least r+2 support points;
   hence at least r distinct interior pivots. The local implicit function
   theorem moves endpoint nodes inward preserving first r moments. These
   pivots plus 0 and 1 imply the CXX separation quantity Delta is positive.
7. A single P0 clears all finitely many small-prime coordinate denominators
   and target denominators. For all multiples P, CXX5.2(iv),(v) applies with
   positive lower bounds independent of P, whereas the error in (i),(iii)
   has a strictly smaller power. Therefore the global rational interior
   solution conclusion follows for every sufficiently large such P.

Extra independent observation: homogeneous local solutions themselves
require exponential K. The unit-indicator polynomial from
p_adic_lower_bound.tex has zero constant coefficient. At any nonzero Q_p
solution to sum_i x_i^j=0 (1<=j<=r), normalize all coordinates into Z_p
with at least one unit. Summing the filter gives zero exactly, so the
positive number of units is divisible by p^floor(r/(p-1)). In particular
K>=2^r at p=2. This supports, but is not needed for, the upper-bound proof.

\begin{proposition}
There are four columns $x_1,\ldots,x_4\in\mathbb Q_3^5$ satisfying
\[
 \frac15\sum_{i=1}^5\prod_{j\in S}x_{j,i}
 =\begin{cases}0,&|S|\text{ odd},\\1/(|S|+1),&|S|\text{ even},\end{cases}
 \qquad \varnothing\ne S\subseteq\{1,2,3,4\}.
\]
In particular, failure of local solubility over $\mathbb Q_3$ cannot
exclude a rational five-row comparison model.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Write $x_j=z_j/3$. The desired integral polynomial equations are
\[
 F_S(z):=\sum_{i=1}^5\prod_{j\in S}z_{j,i}-b_S=0,
 \quad b_S=\begin{cases}15,&|S|=2,\\81,&|S|=4,\\0,&|S|\text{ odd}.\end{cases}
\]
Take the following four initial columns:
\[
 z_1=(1,7,3,15,1),\quad z_2=(19,25,3,15,19),
 \quad z_3=(2,15,7,11,19),\quad z_4=(5,11,15,0,23).
\]
Order the nonempty subsets first by size and then lexicographically.
The residual vector is
\[
\begin{split}
(27,81,54,54,432,297,135,945,837,702,\\
5562,2592,1917,12933,38232),
\end{split}
\]
so every entry is divisible by $27$. Flatten the variables column by
column, using indices $0,\ldots,19$. The Jacobian minor using all
15 equations and variable indices
\[
0,1,2,3,5,6,7,8,10,12,15,16,17,18,19
\]
has determinant $13966477728153600$, of 3-adic valuation one.
These integer calculations are reproduced by
\texttt{lift\_general\_q3.py}, and recorded in
\texttt{general\_q3\_hensel\_certificate.json}.

Fix the other five coordinates. If $J$ denotes this square Jacobian
at the displayed point, its inverse has entries in $3^{-1}\mathbb Z_3$
by the adjugate formula. On the ball $z+9\mathbb Z_3^{15}$ the map
$y\mapsto y-J^{-1}F(y)$ preserves the ball: its displacement at the
center lies in $9\mathbb Z_3^{15}$, and its derivative has entries in
$3\mathbb Z_3$. It is therefore a strict contraction on this complete
ball. Its fixed point is an exact zero of $F$, proving the assertion.
\end{proof}
This is a local-solubility certificate only. It asserts neither a
rational point nor a monotone real point, and does not address other
primes or the realization of a local model by actual dice.

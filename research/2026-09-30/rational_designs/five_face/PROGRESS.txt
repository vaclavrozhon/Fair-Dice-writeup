PROVED, pending a second review:
The reflection-paired ansatz (two column pairs exchanged by row
reflection and negation) has no rational solution, even without
monotonicity. If both even corrections are nonzero, the even mean-zero
rowspace has dimension two and forces fourth moment 1/9 instead of1/5.
If one correction vanishes, rational odd scalar variance1/3 would
require r^2+s^2=5/6, impossible at3. Full proof in
reflection_pair_obstruction.tex.
